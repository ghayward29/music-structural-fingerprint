Artist Structural Similarity

      

        Compares the structural features of this piece — interval patterns, voice leading,
        harmonic rhythm — against fingerprints built from MIDI files by 15 major artists.
        A high similarity score means this piece shares compositional patterns with that
        artist's known work. This is not evidence of copying, but may indicate whose
        structural vocabulary is reflected in the output.
